# About our crawler

> This page is a draft and must be reviewed by the operator and a legal professional before publication (final wording will be applied in F-119).

To help foreign residents in Japan find services available in their language, "Multilingual Service Finder in Japan" (the Service) runs a program (crawler) that checks the supported languages and contact details on websites published by businesses.

- User-Agent: `bizshow-collector/0.1` (includes a link to this page)
- We follow robots.txt. We wait at least 10 seconds between requests to the same site and fetch no more than 3 pages per check.
- We do not access pages that require login and do not submit forms.
- Fetched pages are used only for this check and are deleted within 30 days. We never republish text or images.
- To stop crawling of your site, or to request a correction or removal of a listing, please use our [contact form]({{contactUrl}}). We will not access sites that have asked us to stop.
