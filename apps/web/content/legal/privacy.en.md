# Privacy Policy

> This policy is a draft and must be reviewed by the operator and a legal professional before publication.

[Operator Name] (the "Operator") handles personal information in the Service as follows.

## 1. Information we collect
- Name, email address, message content and similar details you enter in inquiry or listing request forms
- Access logs (IP address, pages viewed, date and time, browser information, etc.)
- Information about the businesses and public institutions listed on the Service (name, address, phone number, website URL, supported languages, etc.). In addition to listing requests from businesses, we may obtain this information from open data published by government agencies and from information that businesses themselves publish on their websites.

## 2. Purposes of use
- Responding to inquiries
- Reviewing listing requests and contacting applicants
- Listing information about businesses and public institutions on the Service so that Users can find services available in foreign languages
- Checking and updating the accuracy of listings
- Preventing misuse and keeping the Service secure
- Statistical analysis to improve the Service

## 3. Disclosure to third parties
We do not provide personal data to third parties without the consent of the person concerned, except as required by law. Among the business information listed on the Service, information about individuals (such as the name of a sole proprietor) is listed only with that person's consent. Information about corporations and public institutions is listed only to the extent that it does not constitute personal information.

## 4. How we obtain listing information and sources
1. The Operator uses open data published by government agencies and others in accordance with their terms of use, and shows the source on each listing page.
2. The Operator may automatically obtain information from websites that businesses themselves publish, in order to check supported languages and contact details. When doing so, we follow robots.txt and other instructions on the site and access it at intervals that do not burden the server. For how we obtain information and how to ask us to stop, see the "About our crawler" page.
3. Data from web pages we obtain is stored temporarily for checking and deleted after a set period (30 days). We never republish the text or images of web pages on the Service.
4. Listings are published after the Operator has checked them, and the date of the check is shown on each listing page.

## 5. Outsourcing
The Operator may entrust the handling of personal information to cloud service providers and similar contractors to the extent necessary for the purposes above, and will supervise them appropriately.

## 6. Security
The Operator takes security measures such as encrypted communication and access control to prevent leakage, loss or damage of personal information.

## 7. Requests for disclosure, correction or deletion
If you request disclosure, correction, suspension of use or deletion of your personal information, we will respond in accordance with the law after verifying your identity. Please contact us using the details below.

A listed business can ask us to correct its listing or stop listing it from "Request a correction or removal of this listing" on each listing page. After confirming the request comes from the business itself, the Operator will in principle respond within 7 days of receipt. Businesses whose listing has been stopped are excluded from further automatic collection.

## 8. Cookies
The Service may use cookies and similar technologies for convenience and analytics. You can disable them in your browser settings.
